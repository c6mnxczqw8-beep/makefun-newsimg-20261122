# The World Today — Sunday 22 November 2026

An entry for make.fun's **news-image-20261122** bounty: one image that sums
up the world's news of Sunday 22 November 2026.

`main.py` copies the bundled `entry.png` (1024×1024, under the 1 MiB cap)
to the output directory, byte for byte, on every run.

## The image

A newspaper-style front page: a masthead naming the day — **SUNDAY
22 NOVEMBER 2026** — over six illustrated panels, each with a short
caption. The artwork is an original editorial illustration generated for
this entry (no news photographs, nothing copied); the masthead and
captions were typeset programmatically so every word is crisp and exact.

## The six stories, and how each panel tells it

| Panel | Story reported on 22 Nov 2026 | In the picture |
|---|---|---|
| Tankers Hit in Hormuz | Bahrain said Iran struck an industrial site as tankers were hit in the Strait of Hormuz | A tanker in a narrow rocky strait, fire burning on the water beside it |
| Missiles Land in Poland | Russian missiles landed in Poland while Europe held military drills nearby | A missile streaking down over farmland toward a village, the Polish flag (white over red) flying |
| COP31 Talks Deadlock | The COP31 climate summit in Antalya, Türkiye, ran past its deadline, deadlocked after a third draft deal dropped any mention of fossil fuels and the EU rejected it | A cracked globe wilting under a blazing sun above an empty conference table |
| Drug Lord Captured | Mexico captured cartel boss Fausto Isidro Meza Flores, "El Chapo Isidro," an FBI most-wanted fugitive, in a deadly firefight | Steel handcuffs and a captured man's silhouette against Mexico's green-white-red |
| US Agencies Hacked | The US Department of Agriculture and the FBI were reported breached by hackers | A monitor showing a cracked padlock, a US government building behind it |
| Indonesia Factory Fire | A deadly fire at a confectionery factory in Indonesia left people dead and dozens missing | A factory in flames, firefighters turning hoses on it |

The selection aims at breadth: the Middle East, Europe, Latin America,
North America and Asia; war, energy, climate, crime, cyber-security and
disaster — the day's most consequential beats, not one country's.
